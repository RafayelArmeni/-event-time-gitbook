# FAQ and troubleshooting

??? question "How do I switch the whole site between dark and light?"

    Open **Theme settings › Colors › Color scheme** and choose **Dark** or **Light**. The three light home templates (summit, webinar and meetup) stay light either way.

??? question "How do I make just one section light on a dark page (or the other way round)?"

    Click the section, open its **Style** tab and set **Section style** to **Light** or **Dark**. See [Section style and the Style tab](../customize/style-tab.md).

??? question "The countdown shows zeros or the "ended" text."

    The date in **Event start date and time** is in the past. Set a future date in the module. Each countdown has its own date.

??? question "My footer columns are empty."

    Choose a navigation menu for **Link column 1** and **Link column 2** in the global footer. See [Header and footer](../getting-started/header-and-footer.md).

??? question "My logo is hard to see."

    The dark pages need a light (white) logo. Set a dark version in the header's **Logo on light pages** field for the light templates.

??? question "The video in "About with video" doesn't play."

    Use a normal YouTube or Vimeo page URL, for example `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...` or `https://vimeo.com/123456789`. Private videos and videos with embedding turned off can't play on other sites.

??? question "The map shows the wrong place."

    Make **Map search** more specific: add the city and country, or use the full street address.

??? question "How do I highlight words in a heading?"

    Wrap them in asterisks: `Where *bold ideas* meet`. See [Highlighted words](../customize/highlighted-words.md).

??? question "Can I use Event Time for more than one event?"

    Yes. Create a separate set of pages for each event (for example under `/2027/` and `/2028/`), or clone your pages and update the content.

??? question "Can I edit the theme code?"

    Marketplace themes can't be edited directly. To change code, clone the theme in **Content › Design Manager**. A cloned theme doesn't receive updates from the marketplace.
