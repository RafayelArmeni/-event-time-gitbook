# Blog

Event Time includes two blog templates:

* **Blog listing**: post cards with an optional topic filter, subscribe form and a call-to-action banner
* **Blog post**: the post with reading time, share buttons, topics, author box, related posts and a ticket banner

## Use them for your blog

1. Go to **Settings › Content › Blog** and choose your blog.
2. Open **Templates** and choose **Blog listing** for the listing page and **Blog post** for posts.
3. Save.

## Turn features on or off

All blog options are in **Theme settings › Blog**:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Show topic filter on the blog listing | On |
| Show reading time | On |
| Show author on post cards | On |
| Show share buttons on posts | On |
| Show topics under posts | On |
| Show author box under posts | On |
| Show related posts | On |
| Show subscribe form on the blog listing | On |
| Show ticket banner under blog posts | On |

## Show recent posts on other pages

Add the **Recent posts** module to any page (the dark Home template already has it) and choose which blog to show.
