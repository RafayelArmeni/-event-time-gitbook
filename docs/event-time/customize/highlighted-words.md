# Highlighted words

Headings can show one or more words in your brand gradient, like "Where **bold ideas** meet the people who build them".

## How to highlight words

Wrap the words in asterisks inside the heading field:

```
Where *bold ideas* meet the people who build them
```

The asterisks aren't shown on the page. You can highlight more than one part:

```
Keynotes, *workshops* and *panels*
```

## Older "Highlighted words" field

Some modules also have a **Highlighted words (optional)** field. It's still supported: type the exact words from the heading there. If the heading contains asterisks, the asterisks win and this field is ignored.

## Change the highlight color

By default highlighted words use the brand gradient. To change it for one section, open the module's **Style** tab › **Custom styles** › **Text colors** › **Highlighted words**.
